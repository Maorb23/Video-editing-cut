Privacy Notice

Last updated: September 8, 2026

This notice explains how Melvid handles personal data when you use its online video-editing service.

Information we collect

We collect the account information you provide, such as your email address; video, audio, images, and editing instructions you upload; rendered outputs; account activity; and records of editing-credit purchases. Payment-card details are collected and processed by Paddle, not by Melvid.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate your account, process and render requested edits, provide support, maintain service security, and administer credits and payments.

Service providers

Melvid uses service providers for infrastructure, storage, processing, and payments. Paddle acts as the payment provider for credit purchases and processes payment information under its own privacy practices.

Retention and security

We retain information only for as long as needed to operate the service, maintain your account, resolve support issues, and meet legal obligations. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may ask about, correct, or request deletion of your account information by contacting us. Some records may need to be retained where required for security, accounting, or legal reasons.
