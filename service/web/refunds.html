Refund Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

This policy applies to one-time Melvid editing-credit purchases processed by Paddle.

Unused credits

You may request a refund within 14 calendar days of purchase when none of the credits from that purchase have been used. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method where possible.

Used credits

Credits used for a successfully rendered final video iteration are non-refundable, because the processing service has been delivered. Planning, review, and preview activity that does not charge a credit is not treated as a used credit.

Technical failures

If a technical problem prevents delivery of a successful final render, contact us within 14 days. We will review the issue and, where appropriate, restore the credit or issue a refund.

How to request a refund

Email maorblumberg@gmail.com from the account email address, including the purchase date and a short description of the issue. Please contact us before opening a payment dispute so we can try to resolve the request promptly.
